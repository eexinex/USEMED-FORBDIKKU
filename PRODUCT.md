# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Patients, doctors, and admins using the system for AI-assisted medical tasks and dashboard management.

## Product Purpose
A Medical AI System that provides a robust, trustworthy dashboard for medical staff to interact with AI diagnostics and manage medical workflows.

## Brand Personality
Clinical, trustworthy, precise.

## Anti-references
Avoid cluttered, overly-complex clinical legacy software. Do not use generic SaaS "cream/warm" aesthetics or playful/consumer-style interfaces.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over ornament**: The interface should be precise and data-forward.
- **Trust through stability**: Predictable, robust layouts that convey clinical confidence.
- **Expert focus**: Streamline workflows without oversimplifying the required data.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard WCAG AA compliance. Focus on legible contrast and clear hierarchy.
